The new /message-board [group] command opens a read-only dialog for one collab group’s retained collab_send history. Switch groups with Tab, filter messages, inspect their text and metadata, and load older entries on demand. The dialog requires Pi’s terminal UI; /messages remains the separate agent-to-agent delivery view.
