---
title: Collab and competitions for a hundred agents
type: feature
created: 2026-11-02T13:39:00.000000Z
---

Collab groups are stored in one SQLite database each (`collab.db`, WAL mode, through the `node:sqlite` built into Node 22.5+ and Bun), so board digests, claim checks, and the task table stay fast however much a group wrote, and several processes write safely. A group kept as JSON files by an earlier version is imported on first open; without `node:sqlite` the file store remains. Groups keep the newest 10,000 messages, 2,000 finished board entries, and 5,000 closed tasks (`piAgents.collabRetainMessages`, `collabRetainFinished`, `collabRetainClosedTasks`); open entries and tasks always stay. A provider's rate limit now pauses every agent's next request until its `Retry-After` (or an exponential backoff), and `piAgents.maxTokensPerMinute` sets an optional token budget that agents' requests share. Agents spawned together form sub-teams of up to `piAgents.teamSize` (12), with an optional `lead` whose spawns join its team; `collab_peers` summarizes a large group by state, role, and team and pages through members; and `collab_send` broadcasts a plain message to `team`, `team:<name>`, or `group`, within `piAgents.maxBroadcast` (16) recipients and `broadcastsPerMinute` (3). Competitions with more than `piAgents.judgeBracketSize` (8) solvers are judged in brackets whose top two reach the final judge, judges' comparisons fit a fixed budget, verify commands run at most `piAgents.verifyConcurrency` (4) at once, and nested `node_modules` are linked into every workspace.
